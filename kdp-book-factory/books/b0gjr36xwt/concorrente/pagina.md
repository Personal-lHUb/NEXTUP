Description (publisher's text, as posted by the author)
In Chart Like a Lawyer, Legal Nurse Consultant and Nurse Practitioner Jaime Weiland shows nurses and NPs how to protect their licenses with legally defensible documentation. Drawing from real-world malpractice cases, she breaks down what attorneys look for in your notes — and what could be used against you.
Learn how to:
• Replace risky phrases like "will continue to monitor" with strong, protective language
• Document critical thinking and clinical judgment clearly
• Avoid common charting pitfalls that lead to lawsuits
• Write notes that hold up under legal scrutiny
Whether you're new to nursing or a seasoned NP, this book will transform the way you document — giving you confidence, clarity, and control over your practice.
